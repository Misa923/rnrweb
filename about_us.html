About R&R Consulting

The people behind the work.

R&R Consulting is a multidisciplinary team of developers, grant writers, writers, and creative professionals who believe that good work starts with understanding the bigger picture.

Our Story

More than technology.

R&R grew out of a long-standing curiosity about how things work.

Our senior developer started working with computers around the year 2000, learning C and Visual Basic. A few years later, in school, he continued with Turbo Pascal. What began as an interest in programming quickly became something much bigger: a fascination with how all the different parts of a system connect to one another and eventually become a product that people can actually use.

That way of thinking has stayed with us. We don't see a website as just a collection of pages, or a database as just a place to store information. We look at how the pieces work together, how people use them, where things become difficult, and how a better system can make someone's work easier.

Over the years, that interest developed into experience with database development, website development, production systems, graphic design, and organizational technology.

But technology is only one part of who we are.

Technology & Creativity

We believe technical work can also be creative.

Our senior developer is also an artist, with approximately 17 years dedicated to watercolor painting and developing a personal artistic style inspired by Mayan Cosmovision.

That artistic background has had a real influence on the way we approach technology and design. It taught us to pay attention to composition, balance, detail, color, meaning, and—perhaps most importantly—the experience of the person looking at what we create.

“Being a developer doesn't mean that everything has to be technical. The best solutions often happen when technology, creativity, and an understanding of people come together.”

Grant Writing

Helping good ideas find the support they need.

Our grant-writing team brings extensive experience working with organizations across the United States since 2013.

We understand that grant writing is not simply about writing something that sounds good. A strong proposal needs to explain what an organization is trying to accomplish, why the work matters, who will benefit, how the program will work, and how success will be measured.

It also needs to make sense financially. The narrative, activities, outcomes, timeline, and budget all need to tell the same story.

That's why our grant writers work beyond the writing itself. We help organizations organize their ideas, strengthen their program descriptions, develop compelling narratives, and present their work in a way that is clear to funders.

Understanding the organization We take time to understand your mission, programs, community, and goals before trying to tell your story.
Building the story We connect the need, proposed solution, activities, outcomes, and impact into a clear narrative.
Connecting the pieces The narrative, program design, outcomes, timeline, and budget should reinforce one another.
Supporting the full process Our experience can support organizations beyond the application, including grant management, reporting, documentation, and program evaluation.
Good communication starts with understanding what you are trying to say.
Copywriting

Making your message sound like you.

We also understand that sometimes the challenge isn't the idea—it's finding the right way to explain it.

Many organizations do important and complicated work. The problem is that the people outside the organization don't always understand it as quickly as the people inside it do.

Our copywriting work focuses on closing that gap. We create content for websites, organizational materials, proposals, reports, marketing communications, and other professional documents.

We don't want your content to sound like it was produced by a generic marketing machine. We want it to sound professional while still sounding like you.

Whether you need to explain a service, tell your organization's story, communicate your mission, or make your website easier for people to understand, we help turn your ideas into clear and meaningful communication.
