Why R&R

Most projects don't fit in one box.

A grant application needs a clean budget section and writing that makes a real case. A new website usually needs a database behind it. A document going to an international partner needs an accurate translation, not a rough guess.

R&R Consulting exists so you can bring the whole project to one place — instead of hiring a developer, a translator, and a grant writer separately and hoping they all understand what the others are doing.

  • One point of contact

    You work directly with the person doing the work — not a rotating account team.

  • Scoped before it starts

    Every engagement begins with a proposal built around what you actually need.

  • Five languages, one team

    Translation is built into how R&R works, not outsourced to a stranger.
